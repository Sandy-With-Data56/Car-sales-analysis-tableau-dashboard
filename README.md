# 🚗 Car Sales Analysis - Tableau Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview
This project analyzes car sales data to identify patterns in **brand performance**, **regional sales distribution**, and the **relationship between customer income and vehicle pricing**.

The dataset was first cleaned and prepared in **Microsoft Excel**, then imported into **Tableau** to build an interactive dashboard.  
The goal is to demonstrate practical skills in **data cleaning, visualization, and business insight generation**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective
Car dealerships and automotive companies need to understand:
- Which brands and regions drive the most revenue
- How customer income influences purchasing decisions

This dashboard was designed to answer key questions:
- Which car brands generate the highest sales?
- Which dealer regions perform the best?
- Is there a relationship between customer income and vehicle prices?
- How do income and car prices change over time?

---

## 🧹 Data Cleaning Process
Before building the dashboard, the dataset required cleaning to ensure accurate analysis.  
Steps performed:
- Removed unnecessary spaces from text columns  
- Standardized car company and model names  
- Corrected mixed values within columns  
- Ensured numerical fields (price, income) were properly formatted  
- Structured the dataset for Tableau analysis  

---

## 🔄 Project Workflow
The dataset was first cleaned and structured in **Microsoft Excel** to ensure accuracy.  
It was then imported into **Tableau** for building interactive visualizations.  
Finally, the dashboard was published on **Tableau Public** and documented here on **GitHub** for sharing and collaboration.

---

## 📈 Dashboard Analysis

### 1. Sales by Car Company
- Chevrolet, Ford, and Mercedes-Benz generate the highest total sales.  
- Audi, Jaguar, and Saab show lower overall sales.  
- A few companies dominate the market in terms of revenue.

### 2. Sales by Dealer Region
- Austin and Janesville generate higher sales compared to Aurora and Middletown.  
- Regional differences may be influenced by population size, demand, or dealership performance.  

### 3. Income vs Price Trend
- Both average income and vehicle prices show a slight decline between 2022 and 2023.  
- This may reflect market conditions or pricing strategy adjustments.  

### 4. Income vs Car Company
- Most purchases occur within a similar income range.  
- Higher-priced vehicles align with higher-income customers.  
- Income level plays a role in purchasing decisions.  

---

## 🔑 Key Insights
- Chevrolet, Ford, and Mercedes-Benz dominate sales.  
- Austin and Janesville are the strongest performing regions.  
- Customer income influences purchasing behavior, especially for higher-priced vehicles.  
- Most purchases occur within a primary target income segment.  

These insights can help automotive companies improve **sales strategies, regional planning, and pricing strategies**.
